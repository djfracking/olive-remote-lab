# Business model recommendation

Pricing snapshot: July 2026. Recheck store rules and comparable prices before launch.

## Launch model

Keep the local web protocol explorer and source repository free. Distribute the native iOS and Android app as a **US $14.99 one-time paid download**. There is no subscription, advertising, account, cloud relay, in-app purchase, or per-server charge.

The paid native app includes:

- Automatic discovery and manual IP connection.
- Device and firmware compatibility check.
- Connection troubleshooting and redacted diagnostics export.
- Full library browsing, search, artwork, and playback controls.
- Now Playing and live progress.
- Multiple saved music servers and easy switching.
- All subsequently verified model adapters and maintenance updates.
- A clearly labeled, offline preview library containing fictional music for evaluation and store review.

Store beta tracks remain free to approved testers. The public store purchase is handled entirely by Apple or Google, so Olive Remote does not need a receipt server or its own account system.

## Price

- TestFlight and Google Play testing: free to approved testers.
- Public launch price: **US $14.99 once**.
- Target regular price after several models are physically verified: **US $19.99 once**.
- Use equivalent store price tiers in other regions rather than manually fixing currency conversions.

At a 15% store commission, the approximate proceeds before tax are $12.74 from a $14.99 sale and $16.99 from a $19.99 sale.

The store listing must name the physically verified model and clearly explain that other models remain evidence-gated. The offline demo helps customers understand the interface but is not a compatibility guarantee. Existing customers keep the app if the price later increases.

## Positioning

The customer is buying convenience and continued use of valuable hardware, not music content. The value proposition is:

> A private, modern remote that gives your existing music server a useful second life.

Public source code is compatible with this model. Technical users can build the project themselves; most customers pay for a signed, tested, automatically updated mobile app and straightforward support.

Comparable legacy-controller apps currently include [iPeng at $8.99](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/ipeng/id767266886) and [SqueezePad at $4.99](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/squeezepad/id380003002). Olive Remote can justify a higher price only through dependable automatic setup, accessible tablet design, current OS support, multi-server switching, privacy, and active maintenance.

## Launch measurement

Track only aggregate store-console information; do not add behavioral analytics to the app. Review:

- Compatibility checks versus completed purchases.
- Refund rate and support requests by model and firmware.
- Store conversion, ratings, and review themes.
- Support time per sale.

Reconsider the price after the first 100 paid customers or 90 days, whichever comes first. Do not introduce a subscription unless the product later gains a real recurring service with recurring cost.
